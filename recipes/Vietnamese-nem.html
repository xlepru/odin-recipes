Vietnamese nem recipe

Ingredients

For the Filling:

  • 450g ground pork (ideally 30% fat for juiciness)
  • 50g bean thread glass noodles (dry weight, soaked in warm water for 15 minutes, then chopped into 2-inch pieces)
  • 30g wood ear mushrooms (dried, rehydrated in hot water for 10 minutes, then finely chopped)
  • 1 medium carrot (finely grated and squeezed of excess moisture)
  • 2 green onions or shallots (finely chopped)
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • ½ tsp sugar and ½ tsp black pepper

For Assembly & Frying:

  • 20–24 round rice paper wrappers
  • Warm water (with 1 tsp of sugar dissolved in it to help the wrappers turn golden and crispy)
  • Neutral vegetable oil for deep frying

Instructions

  1. Make the Filling: In a large bowl, thoroughly mix the ground pork, chopped glass noodles, wood ear mushrooms, grated carrot, green onions, egg, fish sauce, sugar, and black pepper until completely combined
  2. Prep the Rice Paper: Lightly dip a sheet of rice paper into the warm sugar-water mixture for 2–3 seconds until pliable but still slightly firm. Lay it flat on a clean surface.
  3. Roll the Nem: Place 1.5 tablespoons of filling near the bottom edge of the rice paper. Fold the bottom edge tightly over the filling, tuck in both the left and right sides, and roll tightly away from you all the way to the top. Do not wrap too tightly, or they might burst while frying
  4. First Fry (Blanching): Heat about 2cm of oil in a skillet to medium-low heat (around 150°C/300°F). Fry the rolls for 5–7 minutes until cooked through but still pale. Remove and drain on paper towels.
  5. Second Fry (Crisping): Right before serving, heat the oil to medium-high. Fry the rolls a second time for 2–3 minutes until the skins turn deeply golden and shatteringly crispy.

The Traditional Dipping Sauce

Mix 1 tbsp lime juice, 1 tbsp sugar, 1 tbsp fish sauce, and 4 tbsp warm water in a bowl. Stir until the sugar dissolves, then top with finely minced garlic and Thai bird's eye chiles.
