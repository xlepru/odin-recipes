Vietnamese banh-cuon recipe

Ingredients

For the Batter:

  • 1 cup rice flour (130g)
  • 1 cup tapioca starch (120g)
  • 4 cups water (960ml) at room temperature
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • ½ teaspoon salt

For the Filling:

  • ½ lb ground pork (225g)
  • ½ cup dried wood ear mushrooms (rehydrated and finely chopped)
  • ½ medium yellow onion (finely minced)
  • 1 tablespoon fish sauce
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper

For Serving:

  • Nuoc Cham dipping sauce made with fish sauce, lime juice or vinegar, sugar, water, garlic, and chili
  • Crispy fried shallots
  • Blanched bean sprouts
  • Sliced cucumber
  • Fresh mint or coriander
  • Sliced Chả lụa (Vietnamese pork sausage)

Instructions

  1. Make the Batter: Whisk the rice flour, tapioca starch, water, and salt together in a large bowl until perfectly smooth. Let the batter rest for at least 1 hour. For the best texture, you can leave it overnight in the refrigerator. If you rest it overnight, pour off the clear layer of water on top and replace it with the same amount of fresh water. Stir in 1 tablespoon of vegetable oil before cooking.
  2. Cook the Filling: Heat a little vegetable oil in a pan over medium heat. Sauté the minced onion until fragrant and translucent. Add the ground pork and break it apart while cooking. Add the chopped wood ear mushrooms, fish sauce, and black pepper. Cook for 3–4 minutes until the filling is completely cooked and fairly dry. Set aside to cool.
  3. Prepare the Skillet: Heat an 8-inch or 10-inch nonstick skillet over medium-low heat. Brush a very thin layer of oil onto the surface.
  4. Make the Rice Sheets: Stir the batter thoroughly before making each sheet because the starch settles quickly. Pour a small ladle of batter into the skillet and quickly swirl the pan to spread the batter into a very thin layer.
  5. Steam the Rice Sheet: Immediately cover the skillet with a lid and steam for 30–45 seconds. The rice sheet is ready when it becomes translucent and begins to bubble slightly.
  6. Remove the Rice Sheet: Carefully transfer the delicate rice sheet onto a lightly oiled plate or cutting board.
  7. Fill and Roll: Place a spoonful of the pork filling along one side of the rice sheet. Fold the sides inward and roll it tightly into a cylinder. Repeat with the remaining batter and filling.
  8. Serve: Arrange the banh cuon on a serving plate and top with crispy fried shallots. Serve with Chả lụa, sliced cucumber, bean sprouts, fresh herbs, and warm Nuoc Cham dipping sauce.

The Traditional Dipping Sauce

Mix fish sauce, lime juice or vinegar, sugar, and warm water in a bowl. Stir until the sugar dissolves, then add finely minced garlic and chili. Adjust the flavors to your taste.
