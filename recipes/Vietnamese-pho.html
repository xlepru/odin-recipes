Vietnamese pho recipe

Ingredients

For the Broth:

  • 2 kg beef bones + 500g brisket
  • 2 onions + 100g ginger (halved & charred)
  • Spices: 5 star anise, 1 cinnamon stick, 4 cardamom, 4 cloves, 1 tbsp coriander seeds (toasted)
  • Seasoning: 3 tbsp fish sauce, 1 tbsp salt, 30g rock sugar

For Assembly:

  • 400g flat rice noodles (cooked)
  • 300g beef sirloin (sliced paper-thin)
  • Garnishes: Bean sprouts, Thai basil, cilantro, scallions, lime, chilis, Hoisin, Sriracha

Instructions

  1. Parboil: Boil bones and brisket for 5 minutes. Drain and rinse thoroughly to ensure a clear broth.
  2. Char & Toast: Char onions and ginger in a dry pan. Toast spices for 3 minutes, then place in a spice bag.
  3. Simmer: Combine bones, brisket, aromatics, spices, and 4L water. Simmer gently for 3+ hours, skimming fat. Remove brisket after 1.5 hours and slice.
  4. Finish Broth: Season with fish sauce, salt, and sugar. Strain broth and keep boiling hot.
  5. Assemble: Place noodles, brisket, and raw sirloin in bowls. Pour boiling broth over top to flash-cook the raw beef. Serve with garnishes.
